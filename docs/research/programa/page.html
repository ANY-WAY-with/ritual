Interior Design Project Management Software for Professional Studios

Programa is the interior design software studios choose to run the work: purpose-built project management and specification software for interior designers and architects delivering residential, commercial, and hospitality projects. Studios comparing the best software for interior design choose Programa for one reason: it is the only interior design management software built around the way design work actually happens, not adapted from a generic project management tool. Programa is built around the workflows that actually run a studio: FF&E specification, schedules and spec sheets, client approvals, procurement, and the dozens of small handoffs that turn an idea into a finished space. Interior design firms in 80+ countries use Programa as the best project management software for interior designers, replacing four to six disconnected tools (spreadsheets, email chains, file storage, generic project management apps, and one-off templates) with a single platform built for the way studios actually work.

Schedules and spec sheets, built for the way interior designers actually work

Schedules are the heart of every interior design project, and Programa is the specification software studios choose when they take them seriously. Build from a centralized product library that grows with every project:

  • FF&E schedules with supplier pricing, dimensions, lead times, and order tracking on a single record
  • Finish schedules for paints, tiles, stones, and surfaces, mapped room by room
  • Lighting schedules with fixture specifications, wattage, and supplier details
  • Joinery and millwork schedules with custom dimensions and material callouts
  • Custom schedules for any project type, with columns, templates, and structure you control

Specification happens in real time, so there is no version control problem, no double handling, and no rebuilding the same spec sheet across three platforms. Share schedules with clients, contractors, vendors, or your install team in a few clicks, with the latest data visible to everyone who needs it. Interior design templates speed up new projects without forcing your studio into someone else's structure, and Programa's web clipper turns any product URL into a fully populated spec, with images, pricing, and supplier details captured automatically. Designers searching for the best computer programs for interior design, or for the right computer software for interior designers, have moved on from static PDF spec sheets and Excel cells. Programa is where that next era of interior design software programs lives.

Project management software designed for interior designers, not adapted from elsewhere

Generic project management software was built for software engineers and management consultants. Interior design project management is a different discipline:

  • Client approvals on individual product selections, not on tickets
  • Vendor lead times measured in weeks, not sprints
  • FF&E procurement spanning multiple suppliers and shipping windows
  • Install schedules coordinated with builders, electricians, and trades
  • Creative iteration that loops back through the same documents many times

Programa replaces the tools other industries try to bend into shape for a design studio, including general project management apps, accounting platforms, and bolt-on interior design CRM software. Plan tasks, deadlines, and project phases in Kanban or timeline views. Track time against billable hours and surface true project profitability. Centralize project documents in a folder structure built for the way studios actually file and find work. Whether you are a solo interior designer running residential projects from a home studio, a 10-person commercial firm coordinating hospitality fitouts, or a larger architecture practice managing dozens of concurrent jobs, Programa scales with the work without forcing process compromises.

Client dashboards, mood boards, and approvals that elevate every presentation

Programa's interior design presentation software turns the client conversation from a stack of emailed PDFs into a single shared workspace. A client dashboard gives every client a branded link where they can:

  • View live specifications and schedules updated in real time
  • Review mood boards and design concepts in their own time
  • Comment on individual products and selections
  • Approve options with date-stamped sign-off
  • Access everything on desktop, tablet, or mobile

Mood boards let you collect, arrange, and present visual references without leaving the platform you use to run the project, keeping the creative conversation alongside the operational one. Invoicing and payments are handled inside the same system, so the project that started with a brief and a mood board ends with an invoice paid and a profitable studio. The result is fewer email chains, fewer lost decisions, and presentations that feel as considered as the work itself.

The interior design business software trusted by 6,000+ studios

Interior designers and architects across Australia, New Zealand, the United Kingdom, Europe, and North America use Programa every day to run the business of design.

  • 6,000+ studios managing 50,000+ active projects
  • 80+ countries with daily active users
  • 6 to 10 hours saved per project on administrative work
  • Featured in Architectural Digest, Vogue Living, Dezeen, Dwell, and Inside Out
  • Built by people who have worked in interior design studios, not an outsourced helpdesk

That last point matters every time a real question comes up about a real project on a real deadline. Of the many programs for interior designers on the market, Programa is the interior design business software for studios who treat their work seriously, with the support to match.
