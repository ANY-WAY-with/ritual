## Agreed direction — 2026-11-09

- User selected https://marked.dev/ as the Ritual hero reference, specifically the softly cursor-reactive floating images.
- User selected creative references for the floating imagery for now, rather than product screenshots.
- Retain the existing Ritual headline and contact path while adopting a black background, centered sans-serif type, clear/blurred depth layers, and restrained drift.
- Pointer motion follows the source's measured depth limits (4px / 11px / 26px). Keep the center text fixed.
- Keep the pause control and respect reduced-motion preferences. Touch visitors receive the smaller mobile composition without pointer parallax.
- Creative images are generated illustrations, not evidence of client work or screenshots of the Ritual product.
