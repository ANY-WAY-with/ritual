## Proposed positioning

Lead with an AI creative partner that understands the creator's channel and helps advance the next piece of content. User selected creators across all content formats as the first website audience. Use format-neutral positioning and varied examples. The reviewed implementation evidence is strongest in video/channel workflows; broad positioning must not imply equal implementation maturity or integrations for every format.

Format-neutral hero candidate, following the audience decision:

- Headline: “An AI partner that understands your creative world.”
- Supporting copy: “Explore ideas, research what matters, and shape your next piece—with your voice and audience in mind.”
- Preserve “Good work starts with a ritual” as a secondary brand line.
- CTA destination must follow actual launch/access availability. Avoid implying a working download, signup or autonomous publishing flow without verification.

Keep the timed preset bar as a demonstration. Replace “Search for” with “Ask Ritual to…” and short creative tasks. Preserve its existing transition timing, optical depth and soft pointer response. Each preset should show a coherent task with related creative imagery, a source excerpt, an angle, or a short script fragment. Use safe illustrative examples; distinguish them from live results and customer proof.

Suggested demo states:

1. Find my next idea — related story cues and idea/angle snippets.
2. Find a fresh angle — one subject, several editorial approaches.
3. Research this story — relevant sources and a concise evidence-backed note.
4. Shape the opening — a short hook and outline.
5. Pick up where I left off — context and an in-progress piece.

The last state expresses intended continuity and needs a representative supported flow before public demonstration. Do not claim unlimited/perfect memory, automated publishing, video generation/editing, universal integrations or guaranteed performance.

## Aesthetic options

### Editorial studio — recommended proposal

Warm paper and ink, restrained accents from imagery, large composed type, varied image proportions, fine rules and generous but purposeful whitespace. Keep functional labels in a readable sans; compare an all-sans treatment with limited serif emphasis in a headline or editorial excerpt. Serif and new typography remain candidates, not final brand decisions. Mix open sections and purposeful product panels instead of repeating rounded bento cards.

### Expressive creative companion

Warmer, more personal language; authored illustration or marginalia and a stronger accent. Could make Ritual memorable but needs a repeatable visual vocabulary to avoid decorative clutter. Preserve restrained motion.

### Precise product workspace

Sharper sans typography, denser real interface demonstrations and restrained imagery. Communicates capability quickly, but needs creator-specific examples and art direction to stay distinctive.

## Recommended aesthetic changes to discuss

- Build one coherent image story for each preset rather than unrelated luxury travel/still-life imagery. The existing assets can remain during this discussion.
- Retain the floating motion as the main animated signature; quiet the background once an example becomes the focus.
- Give typography an authored hierarchy: one headline voice, readable explanations, quiet UI labels. Compare restrained serif emphasis with a stronger sans direction on identical content.
- Reduce repeated pill/card containers. Let text, images and one clear product demonstration create rhythm.
- Align the website's warmth and density with the actual app without making the landing page an enlarged dashboard.
- Give the Ritual wordmark an intentional typographic treatment; do not treat the current temporary text mark as an accepted owned logo.
