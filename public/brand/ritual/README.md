# Ritual hero assets

Generated on 2026-11-09 using the built-in ImageGen tool. These are original illustrative creative references, not product screenshots or photographs of company/client work.

- `alpine.webp`: anonymous hiker beside an alpine lake, muted green editorial film photograph.
- `fabric.webp`: orange fabric in a sunlit architectural studio, cinematic material study.
- `coast.webp`: cream vintage coupe on a coastal road, editorial travel film photograph.

The selected PNG outputs were inspected, resized to 768px wide, and encoded as WebP without changing their content. Each image is reused across optical depth layers; the references can be replaced by editing the media array in `src/components/ritual/ritual-hero.tsx`.

`inter-latin.woff2` is Inter's Latin variable subset observed on marked.dev. Inter is licensed under the SIL Open Font License; the license is included as `Inter-LICENSE.txt`, sourced from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rsms/inter/master/LICENSE.txt.

Additional preset imagery (2026-11-09): `sculpture.webp`, `night.webp`, and `desert.webp` are original AI-generated creative studies, optimized from 1536×1024 PNG masters to 768×512 WebP. These expand the timed hero presets; they are illustrative, not client work or product screenshots.
